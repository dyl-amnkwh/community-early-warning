# Interview theme codebook (draft v0.1)

Annotate at the sentence or short-passage level. Two coders per transcript for a validation
subset; report Cohen's kappa and per-theme F1.

| Theme | Definition | Example (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| transportation | Difficulty getting to school (bus, ride, distance, cost) | "The bus doesn't come to my street anymore." |
| housing | Moving, instability, shelter, overcrowding | "We've switched places three times this year." |
| caregiving | Caring for siblings or relatives, work duties | "I get my little brother ready before school." |
| health | Physical or mental health, appointments, sleep | "I haven't been sleeping and my stomach hurts." |
| belonging | Bullying, isolation, feeling unsafe or unwelcome | "Nobody talks to me at lunch." |
| food | Food insecurity | "Sometimes there's nothing for breakfast." |
| disengagement | Boredom, feeling behind, seeing no point | "I'm so behind I don't see why I'd go." |
| family_conflict | Conflict, separation, loss, family stress | "Things have been rough at home." |

Mark `none` when no theme applies. Allow multiple themes per passage.
